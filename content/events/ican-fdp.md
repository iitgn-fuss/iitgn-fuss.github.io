faq:
  - q: "What is the fee for participating in the ICAN FDP?"
    a: "There is no registration fee for faculty participants. The ICAN FDP is a national capacity-building program supported by academic consortium partners and government initiatives."
  - q: "Who is eligible to participate in Phase 1 (Online)?"
    a: "Phase 1 is open to all faculty members from engineering and technical institutions across India. A foundational knowledge of computer science, networks, or programming is recommended."
  - q: "How are participants selected for Phase 2 (Residential Phase at IIT Gandhinagar)?"
    a: "During Phase 1, participants complete assignments after each lecture. Faculty members who achieve a Distinction Grade in these evaluations will be evaluated, and the top 25–30 faculty will be invited to the in-person residential phase at IIT Gandhinagar."
  - q: "Will accommodation and food be provided during the residential phase at IIT Gandhinagar?"
    a: "Yes. Accommodation support and hospitality on the IIT Gandhinagar campus will be provided by ICAN & IIT Gandhinagar for all invited Phase 2 participants."
  - q: "Will I receive certificates for completing the program?"
    a: "Yes. Participants who achieve distinction in the online assignments receive an official Certificate of Participation for Phase 1. Participants who complete the residential training at IIT Gandhinagar receive an Advanced Residential Training Certificate."
  - q: "Can I use the lecture slides and lab modules in my own college courses?"
    a: "Absolutely! A core objective of the ICAN initiative is empowering faculty to introduce these materials, syllabi, and hands-on lab experiments directly into their home institutions."
