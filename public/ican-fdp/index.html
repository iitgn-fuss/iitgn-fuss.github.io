    ICAN Faculty Development Program (FDP) on Cybersecurity

    Registration Deadline: Saturday, 28th November 2026
    Hosted this year at IIT Gandhinagar.
    Empowering Educators. Strengthening Cybersecurity Education.

    The ICAN Faculty Development Program (FDP) is a national initiative aimed at upskilling faculty from engineering institutions across India in both foundational and emerging topics in cybersecurity. By empowering educators with the necessary knowledge and resources, we aim to strengthen the cybersecurity curriculum and foster the next generation of cybersecurity professionals.

    This program offers a unique blend of lectures, hands-on labs, and curated resources led by some of the best cybersecurity faculty in the country.

    ICAN

    The Indian Cybersecurity Academic Network (ICAN) has been launched as a collaborative academic effort across IITs, IISc, and other premier institutes. At its core, ICAN seeks to build capacity and strengthen research by preparing faculty from all over India, who will in turn help mould the next generation of cybersecurity leaders for India.

    Program Structure

    Phase 1: Online (Precursor Phase)

    Dates: 1st – 6th December 2026

    Open to all registered faculty members. It serves as both a knowledge-building stage and a selection round for the residential phase.

    Format:
    • Expert-led lectures (2 hours each)
    • Assessment at the end of the phase
    Certification & Selection:
    • Participants who score a Distinction Grade in the assessment receive a Certificate of Participation for the online phase
    • Top 25–30 performing faculty members will be selected for the Residential Phase at IIT Gandhinagar

    Phase 2: Residential (Advanced In-Person Training)

    Dates: 27th – 31st December 2026
    Mode: To be held in person at IIT Gandhinagar

    This intensive, in-person training phase focuses on advanced concepts and practical skill-building. Selected participants engage in a mix of expert lectures and hands-on lab sessions.

    Format:
    • Morning: in-depth lectures by faculty experts
    • Afternoon: lab sessions aligned with the morning topics
    • Each faculty expert will conduct a 2–3 hour lecture + lab session
    Resources & Support:
    • All teaching materials and lab exercises will be made freely available for use in home institutions
    • Accommodation support will be provided by ICAN

    Program Curriculum

    • AI in Cybersecurity
    • Cloud Security
    • Security Threats, Policies and Compliance
    • Infrastructure Security
    • Security of AI Systems
    • Software Security

    Why Participate?

    • Learn from leading experts in cybersecurity from IITs
    • Gain access to ready-to-use teaching materials and lab modules
    • Network with peers and build academic collaborations
    • Receive a certificate of participation and performance-based recognition
    • Opportunity to contribute to shaping the future of cybersecurity education in India

    Who Can Apply?

    • Faculty members from engineering colleges across India
    • Basic familiarity with computer science or IT is recommended
